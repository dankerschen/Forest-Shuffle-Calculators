Players & expansions

Expansion visibility is controlled in the title panel. Hidden cards keep their quantities and continue to score; all-player totals always include them.

Entering a forest

Count visible halves only. Mark qualifying copies, fully occupied trees, and bee hosts. For Wood Ant, mark every bottom card attached to a tree. Silver Fir uses the number of attached cards, not occupied slots. Mistletoe turns its host into a Sapling: remove the original tree from the entered counts.

Symbol totals below must be entered manually, including matching dwellers. This quantity-based page does not reconstruct physical placement or enforce every placement rule.

Visible symbols & expansion markers

Count printed symbols on all visible cards in your forest, including the scored card itself. Ignore hidden halves, cards in your cave and saplings. Do not add virtual symbols for Violet Carpenter Bees.

Mixed printing / expansion-marker adjustments

Some familiar species have additional expansion-marked printings. Adjust the automatically counted marker totals here when your actual visible symbols differ. Positive values add markers; negative values subtract them. These adjustments affect Stone Pine and European Wildcat, not tree species.

Cave

Bearded Vultures score the stored card count separately. Collectors’ Cave doubles only the cave’s own points.

Additional promos / manual correction

This page includes the rules-derived base-game and expansion catalog. Other promos are not certified. Enter independently calculated corrections here, including any dependent scores; a manual adjustment does not add card types or symbols automatically.

All scores / BG Stats

First listed player is exported as starting player. Tied highest scores are shared victories. Review the imported play in BG Stats before saving.
